About Niko

A high standard, lightly held.

Niko Polis works in technology at Microsoft and lives in New Jersey. Outside work, he is serious about training, mental focus, adventure, gaming, and the kind of House and EDM music that can change the energy of an entire day.

His approach is straightforward: earn confidence through consistent action, stay humble enough to keep learning, and leave people feeling better than you found them. He shares the lessons that work for him without pretending there is only one way to grow.

The Niko Standard

What matters, every day.

01

Earn your confidence

Train, learn, recover, and repeat. Confidence lasts longer when there is evidence behind it.

02

Protect your focus

Calm is a competitive advantage. Put attention where it can actually improve the outcome.

03

Bring good energy

Ambition and kindness are not opposites. Make the room lighter without making yourself smaller.

04

Help more than expected

Real leadership is useful. Share what you know, encourage people honestly, and follow through.
